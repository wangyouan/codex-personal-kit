# Verified economics, finance, and accounting referee comments

Use only for a requested review, revision plan, or referee response. This reference adapts the evidence-location discipline of [Barrios econ-referee](https://github.com/Barrios88/barrios-skills/tree/d50afc62f4c67535a1949d029dfa0462feb906fd/skills/writing-and-review/econ-referee). It does not prescribe manuscript edits or expand a polishing request into a review.

For each major comment, retain:

```text
Severity: Critical | Major | Minor
Topic: Contribution | Data | Identification | Inference | Interpretation
Location: section, table/panel/column, figure, appendix, or known page
Evidence: the actual result, construction rule, or statement supporting the concern
Issue and consequence: what is wrong or unresolved and why it matters
Action: a specific clarification, correction, or analysis that addresses this concern
Status: Verified | Unverified (and the missing exhibit needed)
```

Separate verified errors from disputed interpretation, missing documentation, and optional improvements. Do not invent coefficients, sample sizes, citations, page numbers, completed tests, or probabilities of acceptance. A reviewer is a simulated perspective, not a real editor or an editorial forecast.

Start from the author's central question and claim. Check text/table consistency, samples and units, identification, inference, construct validity, magnitude, and literature positioning as relevant to the actual design. An archival accounting paper need not be a DiD study. A descriptive contribution need not be rewritten as causal.

Useful field-specific questions include:

- Accounting: does a disclosure/accrual/audit proxy measure the construct claimed; is regulatory/effective timing aligned; which auditor/office/firm dependence matters?
- Finance: do event timestamps, return horizons, risk adjustment, delisting and sample selection match the claim; are trading costs and multiple testing relevant?
- Corporate finance: are controls post-treatment or otherwise endogenous; is shock-level clustering appropriate; do FE remove the identifying variation?
- Text measures: is the score validated on source excerpts; does boilerplate or a dictionary change drive the result?

Keep editing notes separate from substantive comments. A revision plan may map `priority, comment_id, action, needed_evidence, dependency, verification_status`; scale its detail to the requested review mode. Do not demand additional tests that have no connection to a stated concern.

For a response letter, map each reply to an actually completed revision and verified location. For a re-review, verify that the revised exhibit resolves the original concern rather than merely checking that the author claims it does.
