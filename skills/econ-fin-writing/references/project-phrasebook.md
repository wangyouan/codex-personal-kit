# Project phrasebook

Use when a manuscript matches one of the source author's recurring projects. This file records framing patterns for motivation, identification, mechanism, and contribution. Write fresh sentences and verify every design detail against the manuscript.

The “identification” entries are diagnostic menus, not recommended claims. Never add a design, test, result, or identifying assumption that the project does not actually have.

## Stock-market liberalization, antitrust, and political economy

- **Motivation:** treat market opening or enforcement as a change in firms' competitive, financing, or monitoring environment; connect it to resource allocation and political influence.
- **Identification questions:** staggered program inclusion, index additions, enforcement events, or plausibly quasi-random regulator/judge variation; examine concurrent reforms and endogenous exposure.
- **Contribution:** distinguish monitoring from rent-seeking or financing channels and explain which margin the design can identify.
- **Register:** finance; avoid calling a policy shock exogenous without evidence.

## Cross-border financing liberalization（内保外贷）and real outcomes

- **Motivation:** connect the reform to external-financing frictions and real investment or innovation.
- **Identification questions:** policy pilots, staggered adoption, treatment exposure, selection into the channel, and other credit reforms.
- **Contribution:** separate the specific cross-border channel from general credit expansion; study heterogeneity by ex-ante constraint only when it is pre-determined.
- **Register:** English finance or《金融研究》《世界经济》; in Chinese, use the quasi-natural-experiment framing cautiously.

## Trade war and executive turnover

- **Motivation:** trade-policy uncertainty may propagate from product markets to governance and managerial labor.
- **Identification questions:** tariff exposure, announcement timing, exposure-intensity designs, demand shocks, and placebo industries.
- **Contribution:** move beyond direct trade outcomes to an under-studied governance margin.
- **Register:** finance; define how exposure is constructed before labeling a design exposure-weighted.

## Board gender diversity and corporate outcomes

- **Motivation:** diversity may add complementary information and networks while also changing coordination or conflict; the sign may be theoretically ambiguous.
- **Identification questions:** quotas, mandates, staggered reforms, assignment mechanisms, selection into boards, and timing.
- **Contribution:** distinguish a causal effect from correlation and test a mechanism beyond representation.
- **Register:** finance/management, especially Management Science; foreground the genuine tension and then the design.

## Unionization, employee satisfaction, and physician unionization

- **Motivation:** unionization changes bargaining, employment relations, disclosure incentives, and the distribution of surplus.
- **Identification questions:** close union elections, dues or subsidy reforms, staggered certification, timing of reviews, manipulation near thresholds, and pre-trends.
- **Contribution:** frame incidence clearly—who gains, who pays, and on which outcome margin.
- **Register:** choose by venue: economics for incidence and labor-market questions, finance for firm value or employee-satisfaction consequences, accounting for reporting and disclosure.

## H-1B policy and innovation

- **Motivation:** access to high-skilled immigrant labor may affect innovation and the composition of human capital.
- **Identification questions:** cap lotteries, cap changes, firm exposure, endogenous labor demand, worker reallocation, and spillovers.
- **Contribution:** separate crowd-in from substitution and clarify whether the estimand is firm-level, worker-level, or aggregate.
- **Register:** economics or finance; use “lottery-induced variation” only for a true lottery design.

## Data factors and the digital economy（《经济研究》）

- **Motivation:** connect data and digital transformation to productivity, allocation, innovation, or high-quality growth; avoid treating the policy slogan as the research question.
- **Identification questions:** pilot zones, multi-period DiD, policy selection, concurrent pilots, and pre-trends.
- **Measurement:** validate BERT/LLM or other text-based indicators against human labels, external outcomes, and alternative dictionaries.
- **Contribution:** separate measurement innovation, causal evidence, and mechanism. Distinguish quantity expansion from quality improvement when the data permit.
- **Register:** 中文经管；可用“以……试点为准自然实验”“构建……指标”“增量与提质”，但必须与实际方法一致。

## AI investment and corporate performance

- **Motivation:** AI is a general-purpose technology that may affect productivity, task allocation, competition, and incentives.
- **Identification questions:** text-based adoption measures, exposure indices, deployment shocks, reverse causality, and endogenous disclosure.
- **Contribution:** identify when adoption helps or backfires and test a mechanism rather than report an adoption–performance correlation.
- **Register:** finance/management; distinguish measured adoption, exposure, and actual deployment.

## Carbon tax, environmental protection tax, and greenwashing

- **Motivation:** environmental regulation creates a cost–innovation tension; the Porter hypothesis and compliance-cost view provide competing predictions.
- **Identification questions:** ETS pilots, tax reforms, nonattainment designations, regulated versus unregulated firms, policy selection, and spillovers.
- **Measurement:** distinguish disclosure from real abatement; validate any “say–do gap” or text-based greenwashing measure.
- **Contribution:** separate substantive from symbolic responses and identify supply-chain or externality margins.
- **Register:** English finance/accounting or 中文经管. Use “甄别效应”“实质性与策略性绿色创新” only when the measures support the distinction.

## Political tax avoidance

- **Motivation:** political institutions, ideology, or enforcement can shape corporate tax planning and reputational costs.
- **Identification questions:** judge assignment, elections, agency budgets, enforcement variation, and validity of text-based political-exposure measures.
- **Contribution:** connect a clearly measured institutional input to a corporate tax or financial-policy outcome.
- **Register:** finance/accounting; distinguish tax avoidance, tax aggressiveness, enforcement risk, and realized disputes.

## Cross-cutting identification checklist

When relevant, inspect:

- treatment timing and staggered-adoption estimators;
- event-study dynamics and differential pre-trends;
- anticipation and spillovers;
- treatment selection and concurrent shocks;
- manipulation around RD thresholds;
- instrument relevance, exclusion, and monotonicity;
- shift-share exposure construction;
- textual or machine-learning measure validity;
- economic magnitude and estimand;
- placebo tests tied to actual threats.

Do not request every check mechanically. Explain which threat each proposed test addresses.

After using this phrasebook, convert the framing into the selected field register and run [the naturalness and style audit](ai-fingerprint-defense.md).
